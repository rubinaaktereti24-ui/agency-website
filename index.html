FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a standard project take? +

Typically, our focused landing page sprint is completed and ready for launch in 2 to 3 weeks. Full brand & multi-page platform overhauls take between 4 to 8 weeks depending on scope and iteration velocity.

Do you provide clean and editable source code? +

Yes, absolutely. You receive 100% hand-crafted, semantic, well-documented HTML5 and modern CSS3 code. There are zero heavy framework dependencies or messy compiled files, making it effortless to integrate into any backend, CMS, or hosting environment.

Can you adapt to our existing brand style? +

Certainly. We frequently integrate with established brand guidelines and design systems, elevating them for web interactivity while maintaining rigorous brand compliance.

What if I need ongoing changes after delivery? +

All projects come with standard post-launch warranty support. Additionally, we offer flexible month-to-month retainers for continuous feature evolution, marketing experiments, and design maintenance.
